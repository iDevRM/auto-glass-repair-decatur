Windshield Replacement

The full job: old glass cut out, the urethane bed trimmed back and inspected, any rust on the pinchweld treated, primer applied, and new glass set in a single continuous bead. You leave with a stated safe drive-away time rather than a shrug.

You need this when the damage is in the driver’s viewing area, runs to the edge of the glass, or is long enough that resin will not hold it. Driving on a compromised windshield is worse than most people assume — the glass is bonded structure that helps the roof resist crushing in a rollover and gives the passenger airbag something to deploy against.

Windshield Chip & Crack Repair

A vacuum-and-resin repair on a chip smaller than a quarter, in about half an hour, on your driveway. The resin bonds the fracture so it stops travelling and takes most of the visible damage with it.

This is the job to catch early, and the reason is financial as much as practical: most insurers waive the deductible on a repair but charge it in full on a replacement. A chip left through one cold snap becomes the expensive version of the same problem.

ADAS Calibration

If there is a camera mounted behind your windshield, it drives lane-keep assist, forward collision warning, automatic emergency braking, adaptive cruise and traffic sign recognition. Replace the glass and that camera has to be recalibrated to the vehicle.

Static calibration uses printed target boards at measured distances in a level indoor bay. Dynamic calibration is a road drive at set speeds with a scan tool connected. Some vehicles need both — Honda Sensing, Subaru EyeSight and several Mercedes systems commonly do. A shop that quotes a suspiciously cheap windshield on a camera-equipped car is usually leaving this out.

Door & Back Glass Replacement

Side and rear windows are tempered, not laminated, so they do not crack — they let go all at once into thousands of blunt pieces. Most of those pieces end up inside the door cavity, under the seat rails and in the carpet.

The replacement itself is straightforward. The part that separates a good job from a bad one is pulling the door panel and vacuuming the cavity properly, because glass left in a door rattles, jams the regulator, and works its way out for months.
