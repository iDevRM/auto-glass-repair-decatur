What Happens When You Call?

1. Two minutes on the phone

Year, make, model, and where the damage sits. That tells us whether it is a repair or a replacement, whether your car has a camera behind the glass, and what the job costs.

2. We book a window

You tell us where the car will be parked. We need a level spot and room to open a door fully — a driveway or an office lot both work.

3. Done on site

Chip repairs take about half an hour. Replacements take an hour or two plus cure time, and you get told exactly when the car is safe to drive.
